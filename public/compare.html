AlgoTest vs StockMock

Both are Indian options backtesting platforms — but they diverge sharply once you move past backtesting.

AlgoTest — Full-cycle algo trading platform
StockMock — Backtesting & simulation focused
⚡

AlgoTest takes your strategy the full distance — backtest → paper trade → live auto-execution across 60+ brokers, all in one platform.

🔬

StockMock is laser-focused on expiry-based backtesting and simulation. It stops at paper trading — no live execution, no broker integration.

🎯

If you only need to test and learn strategies, StockMock is simpler and cheaper. If you want to actually run them live, only AlgoTest does that.

₿

For crypto (BTC/ETH), only AlgoTest supports it via Delta Exchange. Neither platform supports Forex (EURUSD) or Gold.

AlgoTest — Choose if you want to…

  • Go from backtest all the way to live automated trading in one tool
  • Connect to your broker and auto-execute without lifting a finger
  • Build indicator-triggered strategies using Signal AI
  • Analyse Greeks, run Monte Carlo tests, and stress-test your edge
  • Trade 500 NSE stock options, not just index options
  • Backtest or live-trade BTC / ETH crypto strategies
  • Run side-by-side comparisons of multiple strategies
Best for: Traders ready to automate — who need the full pipeline from idea to live execution.

StockMock — Choose if you want to…

  • Test expiry-based options strategies with zero cost
  • Learn how straddles, strangles, iron condors perform historically
  • Simulate strategies in a live market environment before risking money
  • Use leg-wise stop-loss and profit lock features
  • Have a simple, focused tool with a flat low monthly fee
  • Work on a desktop app (Windows / Mac)
  • Get started quickly without a steep learning curve
Best for: Beginners and learners who want to test expiry strategies and understand options before going live.

Key Takeaways
⚡

AlgoTest is VC-backed and scaling — YC + angels give it resources to hire (24–46 staff) and expand features fast.

🧑‍💻

StockMock is a 1-person company generating ₹5.88Cr/year in revenue — arguably more capital-efficient per employee than AlgoTest.

📊

AlgoTest has 2× more traffic (global rank #46K vs #103K), but StockMock users spend less time — indicating it's used as a quick check-and-go tool.

🎯

AlgoTest users are far more engaged — 8.6 pages/visit and 7+ min sessions vs StockMock's 5.6 pages and 3:48. Deep feature usage vs quick backtests.

📉

Both saw traffic dips recently (−9% and −8% MoM). Likely seasonal — Indian markets were slower in that period.

🏆

StockMock ranks higher in its category (#208 Finance/India) than AlgoTest (#961) despite lower traffic — it dominates a narrower, more specific niche.
