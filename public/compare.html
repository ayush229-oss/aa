AlgoTest vs StockPro

A feature-by-feature breakdown of two popular Indian trading platforms — one built for automation, one built for education.

AlgoTest — Algo Trading Software
StockPro Online — Trading Education Academy
⚡

AlgoTest is a fully automated algo trading platform. You build, test and deploy strategies — no mentor, no classes, no hand-holding.

🎓

StockPro is a structured learning academy. You attend live sessions, get a mentor, and learn to trade manually — no code, no automation.

🎯

If your goal is automated strategies, AlgoTest wins outright. If your goal is learning to read the market, StockPro is more relevant.

🔄

They serve different purposes — you could realistically use both: learn on StockPro, then automate on AlgoTest.

AlgoTest — Choose if you want to…

  • Build rule-based options strategies without writing code
  • Backtest against 7.5+ years of NSE historical data
  • Run Monte Carlo and scenario stress tests on your strategy
  • Paper-trade before going live with real money
  • Deploy fully automated trading across 60+ Indian brokers
  • Analyse Greeks, payoff curves, and IV in real time
  • Already know how to trade and want to systemise it
Best for: Systematic traders, quants, and experienced options traders who want to automate strategies.

StockPro — Choose if you want to…

  • Learn the fundamentals of technical and options trading from scratch
  • Get live mentorship and guidance from experienced traders
  • Attend structured live trading sessions (20+)
  • Find breakout stocks using ready-made proprietary scanners
  • Earn a certified qualification in stock market trading
  • Learn intraday, swing, positional, and index options trading
  • Become part of a guided cohort with ongoing doubt support
Best for: Beginners and intermediate traders who want structured education and mentored live trading experience.
